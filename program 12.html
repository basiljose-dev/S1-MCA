Paridise Resort

Paradise Resort is a beautiful and relaxing destination surrounded by nature, offering guests a comfortable and peaceful stay. Located in a scenic area of Kerala, the resort provides modern rooms, delicious dining options, Wi-Fi, parking, and recreational facilities for a pleasant holiday.

Guests can enjoy the refreshing natural surroundings and explore nearby attractions, including picturesque landscapes, waterfalls, lakes, and other popular sightseeing spots.
